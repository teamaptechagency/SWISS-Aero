ABOUT US

A small firm that answers the phone.

Swiss Aero was founded in London to give small business owners the kind of attention usually reserved for large clients.

How we work

Every client is assigned one qualified accountant — the same person you email, call and meet. No ticket queue, no handing your file between departments.

We file to HMRC and Companies House on your behalf, monitor your deadlines before they arrive, and tell you what a decision costs before you make it.

Fees are fixed and quoted up front. If your business changes shape, we agree the new fee with you first.
